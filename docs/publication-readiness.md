## Supplier conclusion

Google Workspace's CDPA acceptance is confirmed. Tally's published professional-user terms incorporate its DPA for the owner-confirmed business use of the free account. Cloudflare's published DPA forms part of its self-serve agreement, and the owner confirms the UK company billing account. Tally's direct Belgium transfer has UK adequacy; Google's CDPA and Cloudflare's DPA describe safeguards for applicable restricted transfers. Account-specific routing locations and onward supplier changes remain ordinary monitoring items, not evidence of an uncovered transfer.

GitHub is the exception. The repository is in the director's personal account. GitHub [documents visitor IP logging for Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) and publishes [controller privacy and international-transfer commitments](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Its [customer DPA scope](https://github.com/customer-terms) does not establish coverage for free Pages in a personal account. The exact role for Pages hosting cannot be settled from these public terms. The website does not place Tally responses or enquiry contents in GitHub, and its privacy notice names GitHub Pages and technical logging. Do not describe GitHub as a contracted company processor or claim a company-specific transfer addendum without confirmation.

The [ICO requires a written contract when a controller uses a processor](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/guide-to-accountability-and-governance/contracts). **The minimum unresolved privacy decision** is for the owner to record whether it can rely on GitHub's published controller treatment of Pages visitor security data for this personal-account site. If the owner determines GitHub acts as a processor for this data, obtain applicable written processing terms or GitHub clarification before launch. No change to the website or additional infrastructure is indicated by the evidence reviewed.
