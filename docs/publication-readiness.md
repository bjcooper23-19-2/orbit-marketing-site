## Processor and transfer review

The website has no registration backend. Tally receives the form response; Google Workspace handles correspondence; Cloudflare handles the domain and email routing; GitHub Pages will serve the static site and may process visitor technical data. Provider terms are published, but public documents alone do not establish the agreements or transfer routes applicable to Nineteen Point Two Limited's actual accounts.

| Service | Published position | Account-specific check still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tally | [Tally says](https://tally.so/help/gdpr) professional account holders accept its [DPA](https://tally.so/help/data-processing-agreement) through account terms; it says form data is stored in Europe. [UK adequacy covers Belgium](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/adequacy-regulations/). | Confirm the professional account is held for Nineteen Point Two Limited and retain the applicable terms and subprocessors with the processing record. | Cloudflare | Its [customer DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) describes processor duties and UK transfer routes, including the UK Addendum where applicable. | Confirm the current account agreement incorporates the DPA and record which domain and email-routing data Cloudflare handles and the applicable transfer route. |
| Google Workspace | Google offers a [Cloud Data Processing Addendum](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/compliance/privacy-compliance-and-records-for-google-workspace-and-cloud-identity), which may already be incorporated in the account agreement or can be accepted in Admin. | Confirm incorporation or acceptance for the Workspace account receiving `hello@` mail, and record its relevant transfer terms. |
| GitHub Pages | GitHub publishes a [Data Protection Agreement](https://github.com/customer-terms/github-data-protection-agreement) with both processor and independent-controller roles for different data. | Confirm the agreement and role applicable to the repository and Pages visitor data. The [ICO requires a written contract when a controller uses a processor](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/guide-to-accountability-and-governance/contracts). A UK restricted transfer must have [adequacy, appropriate safeguards or an applicable exception](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/a-brief-guide-to-international-transfers/). The owner should record the applicable terms and route for each actual data flow before public collection begins. This is the remaining contractual publication gate; it is a records and account review, not a request for new website infrastructure.
