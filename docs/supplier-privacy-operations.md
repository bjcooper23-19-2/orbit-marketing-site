# Internal supplier and privacy operations record

Recorded 25 November 2026 for Nineteen Point Two Limited. Owner confirmations below are recorded as supplied; provider account terms and settings were not independently inspected. This is an internal operating record, not a legal opinion.

## Scope and data flows

The marketing site is static and informational. It links to an external Tally form that collects an email address and explicit consent for Orbit updates. Enquiries arrive at `hello@myfamilyorbit.uk`. The site does not collect household records, create Orbit application accounts or take payments. Its own code contains no analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages will serve website files; GitHub and Cloudflare may process visitor technical data. Application data and its suppliers are outside this record.

## Operating responsibility

The company director is responsible for monitoring `hello@myfamilyorbit.uk`, privacy and deletion requests, consent withdrawals and retention reviews. Tally submission and consent recording, owner notifications, the thank-you experience and inbound email delivery have been tested. The ICO data protection fee has been paid.

Use the existing Tally account and mailbox for a manual process: record the receipt and outcome of a request privately, verify the requester as needed, locate the relevant Tally or email record, stop requested updates, export or delete data as appropriate, and respond within the applicable deadline. Do not place personal data or consent exports in this repository. Review the registration list at least annually and remove records no longer needed; normally delete resolved general enquiries within 12 months, subject to any necessary legal hold. Keep the minimum suppression record needed to respect withdrawals.

Before sending any Orbit update emails, include a simple opt-out in each message and check the suppression record. This is a **pre-send** requirement, not a condition for publishing the static site. Check actual cookies and storage on the deployed Pages domain during release acceptance; optional analytics or advertising technology would need separate review and any required consent before activation.
