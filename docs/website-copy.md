# Website copy

The approved master brief supplied for this build is the copy source. The page templates in `src/site.mjs` are the implemented copy. Core wording and its order follow that brief; small changes keep capability claims in line with product validation.

- Home: what Orbit does, a school-trip example with genuine product screens, the calendar distinction and founder connection.
- Why Orbit: the less visible work before a calendar entry or task exists, and why Orbit starts with incoming information.
- How it works: Capture → Reduce → Sort → Clear through one school-trip example and genuine demo screens. The fuller evening-before view remains labelled as a goal under development.
- Our story: Ben's first-person founder letter, with the supplied photograph.

The site does not claim that Orbit submits forms, pays fees, automatically makes family decisions or fully supports every household configuration today. Registration leads to the configured Tally form for email updates, testing opportunities and future availability; it does not promise early access. Review any future copy change against the product brief and the current app revision.
