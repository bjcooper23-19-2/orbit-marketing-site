# Website copy

The approved master brief supplied for this build is the copy source. The page templates in `src/site.mjs` are the implemented copy. Core wording and its order follow that brief; small changes keep capability claims in line with product validation.

- Home: what Orbit is, where family information comes from, the move to a clearer plan, shared understanding, inclusive ambition and founder connection.
- Why Orbit: the less visible work before a calendar entry or task exists.
- How it works: Capture → Reduce → Sort → Clear, real demo screens and family situations. “Are we ready for tomorrow?” is labelled as a product goal under development.
- Our story: Ben's first-person founder letter, with the supplied photograph.

The site does not claim that Orbit submits forms, pays fees, automatically makes family decisions or fully supports every household configuration today. Registration and contact are honest unavailable states until real destinations exist. Review any future copy change against the product brief and the current app revision.
