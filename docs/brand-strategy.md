# Brand strategy

Orbit's purpose is to give families less to carry. The public promise is: “Orbit gives parents one place to put the jobs family life creates, so there is less to remember, work out and chase.” This PR tests “From school emails to family plans, without all the admin in between.” as the homepage headline. The approved tagline remains “More family life. Less life admin.”

The audience is adult parents, guardians and carers across varied household arrangements. Use warm, direct British English. Orbit should sound like a parent speaking to another parent: use familiar, everyday words in place of corporate, operational or technical language. Describe family information as fragmented and organisational responsibility as often concentrated. Avoid blaming the person carrying it or the person trying to help. Do not present Orbit as a calendar replacement, generic task manager or AI assistant.

The marketing palette is deep teal `#245B56`, warm ivory `#F7F5EF`, lavender `#CEC8E3`, coral `#E38D77` and deep ink `#293746`. The product's existing teal mark provides continuity. DM Sans carries practical text; upright Newsreader adds editorial warmth. The exact Orbit mark comes from `src/components/OrbitLogo.tsx` in the referenced product revision and the favicon is copied unchanged. The product's implemented CSS, especially its teal and type treatment, took precedence over historical brand notes.

## Heading patterns

Heading tokens and reusable classes live in `src/styles.css`. `heading-mixed` pairs DM Sans with upright Newsreader only across a complete line or clause, as in the homepage and How it works campaign statements. `heading-editorial` sets a whole reflective heading in Newsreader. `heading-sans` keeps a whole explanatory statement in DM Sans. Body copy, navigation, labels and controls remain DM Sans. Choose the pattern for the whole thought before adding line breaks; do not switch faces halfway through a grammatical unit.
