# Creative direction

The approved concept is **The Weight of Responsibility**, with a visual move from complexity to clarity. The asymmetric homepage hero layers forty fictional incoming information fragments against a spacious headline. Email panes, newsletter pages, chat and text threads, invitations, posters, tables, forms, reminders and handwritten lists retain the visual cues of their sources. Uneven clusters carry the pressure, while older items are buried and newer messages crop at the edges. Later sections use calmer spacing, actual synthetic-demo product imagery, a simple responsibility diagram and the founder photograph.

The fragments are fictional examples built with HTML and CSS, not screenshots. The collage is decorative and hidden from the accessibility reading flow. Product captures are genuine tracked images from the Orbit demo and are labelled as such. The founder photograph retains its original framing and natural colour. There is no stock family photography, decorative motion or repeated feature-card grid.

Mobile is intentionally recomposed: the headline and explanation come first, then a dense twenty-six-fragment field. In the founder teaser, the copy sits to the left of the unchanged photograph on desktop so Ben looks into it; mobile keeps copy before the photo in reading order. The page progressively opens into calmer sections. Keep navigation, copy and actions visually clear even where fragments overlap.
