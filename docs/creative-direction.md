# Creative direction

The approved concept is **The Weight of Responsibility**, with a visual move from complexity to clarity. The asymmetric homepage hero gathers uneven school, invitation, message and reminder fragments against a spacious headline. Later sections use calmer spacing, actual synthetic-demo product imagery, a simple responsibility diagram and the founder photograph.

The fragments are fictional examples, not screenshots. Product captures are genuine tracked images from the Orbit demo and are labelled as such. The founder photograph retains its original framing and natural colour. There is no stock family photography, invented app screen, decorative motion or repeated feature-card grid.

Mobile is intentionally recomposed: the headline and explanation come first, then the fragment field. The page progressively opens into calmer sections. Keep navigation, copy and actions visually clear even where fragments overlap.
